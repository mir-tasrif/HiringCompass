# SYNTHETIC SAMPLE - Company Hiring Policy (for development only)

This document is synthetic demonstration content, not a real company policy.

## Selection criteria
Job requirements must relate directly to the duties of the role. Criteria such as age, gender, nationality, religion or marital status must never be used.

## Experience equivalence
Relevant professional experience may substitute for a formal degree at a ratio of two years of experience per missing degree year. Equivalences must be stated in the job profile.

## Mandatory versus preferred requirements
A mandatory requirement is a hard filter and must be clearly justified by the role. Preferred requirements improve ranking but never exclude a candidate.

## Interview process
Every shortlisted candidate for a role receives the same structured interview. Hiring decisions are made by people, and each decision must be supported by recorded evidence.
